## Description – EN
Quran Masterclass is a calm, beautifully designed way to learn the Quran verse by verse – to listen, understand its tafsir and keep it in your heart.

THE SHAMS METHOD
Every verse in seven short steps: listen, build it up backwards, word by word, meaning and memory hooks, tafsir, fading cues with an honest self-check, and reflection. A personal coach plans each verse for you, and spaced repetition brings verses back exactly when they start to fade.

THE WHOLE QURAN
Read and listen to all 114 surahs with translation, transliteration and word-by-word meanings. The word being recited lights up. Classical tafsir (Ibn Kathir abridged, Ma'arif al-Qur'an and more) with its source under every text.

LEARN AND PRACTISE
Academy tests for every lesson, Arabic reading from the first letter, a Tajweed course, vocabulary cards, learning to pray and wudu step by step.

EVERY DAY
Prayer times for your place with a live Qibla compass and reminders (Sunni and Shia calculation), a Quran radio that keeps playing on the lock screen, duas from the Quran and the Sunnah, a Hijri calendar and a khatm planner.

YOUR PROGRESS
Today's plan, the Quran map, statistics, achievements – synced with your account on quranmasterclass.com.

FREE AND PRO
Free forever: the whole Quran with translation and tafsir, the Shams Method for al-Fātiḥa and Juz ʿAmma, prayer times, Qibla, duas and much more.
Quran Masterclass Pro unlocks the Shams Method for all 114 surahs, all reciters and radio stations, the complete Arabic and Tajweed courses, Academy tests for every surah, detailed statistics, the AI assistant and five personal duas a month.

Pro is a monthly auto-renewable subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings. Personal duas can also be bought one at a time.

Quran Masterclass is a learning app; it does not replace a qualified teacher or scholar.
